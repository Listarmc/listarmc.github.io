Call of the Night GUI Resource Pack

Transform your Minecraft interface with the dark, mystical aesthetic of Call of the Night. This pack brings a stunning dark purple, pink, and light blue color palette to your Minecraft experience, perfect for fans of both Minecraft and this captivating supernatural romance anime.

The Call of the Night Custom Minecraft GUI Pack is a GUI-only resource pack featuring a dark purple, pink, and light blue color palette inspired by the anime's aesthetic. This pack includes scaled containers that required significant re-adjustments for optimal visual balance, making it distinct and visually engaging.
