Dandadan GUI Resource Pack

Enhance your Minecraft gameplay with the Dandadan Custom GUI Pack. This resource pack brings the vibrant and dynamic visuals of the anime "Dandadan" to your game, offering a customized interface that can be layered over other resource packs for a truly unique and immersive experience.

This GUI-only pack integrates effortlessly with your existing resource packs—simply place it on top of your current pack. Featuring a striking custom palette of blue, red, and pink, it brings unique, anime-inspired visuals to every container, including the hot bar.

Installation Guide

1

Download the Pack

Click the "View Pack" button on any texture pack to go to its download page.

2

Locate Your Resource Packs Folder

Open Minecraft, click "Options" → "Resource Packs" → "Open Pack Folder".

3

Move the Pack

Move the downloaded .zip file into your resource packs folder.

4

Activate the Pack

In Minecraft, select the pack from the available resource packs and click "Done".
